# Project types and templates

Source: `src/editor/projects/ProjectStore.*`, `src/editor/ProjectInfo.h`, `src/editor/templates/TemplateStore.*`, `templates/`.

## project.json

```json
{ "name": "Sample 2D Platformer", "dimension": "2d", "vr": false,
  "created": "...", "last_opened": "...", "plugins": "..." }
```

- **`dimension`** is `"2d"` or `"3d"`. 2D claims open the 2D editor and 3D claims open the 3D editor. A file with no `dimension` loads as 2D.
- **`vr`** turns a 3D project into a VR project. The legacy value `"dimension": "vr"` loads as 3D with VR on. You can flip VR in the create flow or in **Claim Settings**.
- **`plugins`** is the list of plugins enabled for this project (see below).

Each project is a folder under `projects_root` (default `./TombStoneProjects/`). It holds `project.json`, `scene.json`, `assets/`, `scripts/` and optionally `ui/`.

## Claim templates

**+ New Claim...** opens the template picker (the **Break Ground** dialog). You can filter by All, 2D, 3D, VR or Mine, or search by name, tag or plugin. Cards are grouped:

1. **Empty**: clean starting points with no sample content and no optional plugins.
2. **Samples**: playable sample claims to learn from.
3. **Mine**: your own templates from **Save as Template**.

Each card shows an engine-rendered thumbnail, a description, its dimension and VR badges, and its plugins.

Each template is `templates/<id>/template.json` plus a baked `project/` folder. That folder is copied when you create a claim, and `project.json` is then stamped with the name you picked.

### Empty templates

| id | Dimension | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| `empty-2d` | 2D | A camera, one empty tilemap layer (32 px tiles) and the 2D lighting off. Nothing else. |
| `empty-2d-topdown` | 2D | A top-down player (Player Controller, 4-way move) with `scripts/player.lua`, a follow camera and an empty tilemap. |
| `empty-2d-platformer` | 2D | A player with gravity and jump (Player Controller in **Platformer** mode) and `scripts/player.lua`. Also two rows of solid Stone ground tiles and a follow camera. |
| `empty-3d` | 3D | A camera entity (the Play view), a directional sun, a daylight sky and a ground plane. No props. |
| `empty-3d-fps` | 3D | An FPS controller (Player, **First person** view) with its eye camera as a child, `scripts/player.lua`, a sun, sky and ground plane. |
| `empty-3d-tps` | 3D | A capsule character controller (Player, **Third person**) with the orbit camera and `scripts/player.lua`, plus a sun, sky and ground plane. |
| `empty-vr` | 3D + VR | A VR rig with hands (`assets/hand.gltf`), teleport plus 45° snap turn and `scripts/player.lua`, plus a sun, sky and ground plane. Without a headset, Play rides the rig flat on the desktop. |
| `truly-blank` | 2D or 3D | No entities at all. Pick 2D or 3D with the toggle next to **Break Ground**. |

Every empty template includes a `README.md` (what is in it, how to play it, next steps) and a `project.json` with `"plugins": ""`, so no optional plugins (not even `ts.posse`) are on. Turn plugins on later in the **Trading Post**. The engine has no input-action mapping yet, so the controls are the engine defaults: WASD, the arrow keys or the left stick to move, and Space, E or pad A (the action button) to jump in the platformer. That is why there is no input-actions file. All of the content, including the hand model and thumbnails, is generated by the engine and is CC0.

### template.json

Fields: `id`, `name`, `dimension`, `vr`, `description`, `plugins`, `tags`, `order`, `thumbnail`, `project`, and an optional `generator`. If the `project/` folder is missing, the generator's built-in writer is used instead.

These optional fields go with the groups:

- **`group`** is `"empty"` or `"samples"` (the default). User templates always show under Mine.
- **`alt_dimension`**, **`alt_project`** and **`alt_generator`** give one card a second dimension. `truly-blank` uses them to ship `project/` (2D) and `project-3d/` (3D), and the picker's 2D/3D toggle chooses between them.

Templates are searched in this order:

1. `$TS_TEMPLATES_PATH`
2. `<exe>/templates`
3. The source tree

**Save as Template** writes the current claim into `<projects_root>/.templates/`, where it shows under **Mine**.

### Regenerating and testing

- `ts_admin --bake-templates <dir>` regenerates the baked `project/` folders from the generators.
- `ts_admin --bake-thumbnails <dir>` re-renders every `thumbnail.png` with the engine. 2D thumbnails use the runtime draw list with the claim's plugins loaded. 3D thumbnails use the GL renderer and need a display or Xvfb.
- `ts_admin --templates-shot <png>` saves a screenshot of the New Claim dialog. It uses a temporary projects root and never writes `imgui.ini`.
- `ts_admin --smoke-templates` checks the groups, the thumbnails and that the empty templates have no plugins. It then creates a claim from **every** template (both dimensions of `truly-blank`) in temp folders, opens it in its editor and runs Play headless for a few frames. Checks include:
  - nothing gets seeded;
  - the top-down player walks;
  - the platformer player lands on the ground and jumps;
  - the FPS camera sits at eye height and looks up and down;
  - the third-person camera orbits behind the player;
  - VR snap turn and teleport work in the simulator.

## Empty-template features in the engine

- **2D Player Controller: Platformer mode.** In the inspector, Movement is **Top-down** or **Platformer**. Platformer walks on the horizontal axis and falls with `gravity` (px/s², default 1400). It jumps with `jump_speed` (px/s, default 560) when grounded: press W, Up or the action button (Space / E / pad A). It collides with solid tiles and colliders. In `scene.json` this is `"mode": "platformer"`, plus `gravity` and `jump_speed`, all optional.
- **2D empty scenes play.** A scene with no entities enters Play (an empty ride) instead of failing.
- **3D Player: First person view.** The Player inspector's **View** is **Third person** (orbit camera, default) or **First person**. First person puts the camera at `eye_height`, or on a child entity with a Camera component. Look up and down with right-drag or PageUp/PageDown, clamped to ±85°. JSON: `"view": "first_person"`, `"eye_height"`.
- **3D Camera component.** An entity with `"camera": { "fov": 60 }` is the Play camera when there is no player and no VR. In first person, a child camera of the player is the eye.

## Plugins per project

Turn plugins on or off for a project in the **Outfitter's Wagon** (the Plugins panel, `src/editor/plugins/PluginsPanel.*`). Turning one on brings its dependencies along, and turning one off also turns off its dependents. The list is saved to `project.json` right away. `ts_game`, `ts_server` and `ts_client` load the project's plugins too.

Built-in plugins (`src/plugins/`):

| Plugin | Default | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| `ts.posse` | on | Multiplayer |
| `ts.tumbleweed` | off | Sample gameplay plugin |
| `ts.frontier` | off | Survival layer, `src/plugins/survival` |
| `ts.shipyard` | off | Airship Works ship builder, `src/plugins/shipyard` |
| `sample.lantern` | n/a | Sample dynamic plugin |

The root README documents Frontier Survival and Airship Works in depth.
